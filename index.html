Privacy

Zero data collected.
Ever.

GitBoba is a client — nothing more. It connects your device directly to your server, with no middlemen and no telemetry.

Keychain-secured credentials

Your server URL and API token live exclusively in the iOS Keychain — hardware-backed, sandboxed, and never transmitted anywhere.

Direct connection only

Every network request goes straight from your device to your Gitea instance. No proxy, no relay, no developer-operated servers in between.

No analytics or tracking

Zero analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or ad frameworks. What you do in the app stays entirely on your device.
