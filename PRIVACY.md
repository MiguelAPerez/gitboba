# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 5, 2026**

GitBoba is a client app for self-hosted Gitea servers.

## What we collect

Nothing. GitBoba does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server operated by the developer.

## Your data stays on your device and your server

- **Credentials** (your server URL and API token) are stored exclusively in the iOS/macOS Keychain on your device.
- **All network requests** go directly from your device to your own Gitea server. No data passes through any third-party server.
- **No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking** of any kind is included in the app.

## Third-party SDKs

GitBoba uses no third-party libraries or SDKs. It uses Apple system frameworks only (SwiftUI, URLSession, StoreKit).

## In-App Purchase

GitBoba offers a one-time Pro upgrade via Apple's App Store (StoreKit 2). All purchase handling is done through Apple. The developer receives no financial or personal data directly.
