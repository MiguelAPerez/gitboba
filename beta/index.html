What to expect

  • Installing the beta replaces the App Store copy of GitBoba on that device. Install from the App Store again to go back.
  • Each build stays available for 90 days. TestFlight prompts you when a newer build is ready.
  • A Supporter purchase inside the beta is not charged. It does not unlock Supporter on the App Store version.
